This is the Sheets I use to calculate the intrinsic values of U.S. equities in my portfolio, or any other companies I'm interested in. I typically use a 10-year time horizon and use my own discount rate, since the WACC's calculation uses beta, which measures volatility, which I don't think reflects the true risk of a company.
